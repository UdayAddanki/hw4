1. Storefront Home Page - I made the Home page feel more like a real merchandise storefront while keeping the Campus Customs editorial style. 3. Product Details - I strengthened the product-detail hierarchy so the large product image is prominent and the name, price, colors, sizes, stock, and description are easier to follow. The information still comes from the existing catalogue and inventory data.

4. Shopping Experience - I kept the navigation simple and made the category sections and featured products easier to understand. I also kept the AI shopping assistant, chat product cards, authentication, logout, and product links working with the existing Campus Customs identity.

5. Responsive Design - I kept the layout responsive for smaller screens by stacking collection cards and featured products on mobile. I used subtle hover states and the existing navy, cream, gold, serif, and sans-serif visual language so the site feels polished without becoming flashy.
